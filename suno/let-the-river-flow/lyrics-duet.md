# Let the river flow — duet lyrics (TAYLOR + MILEY voices)

For style **E-duet** in `styles-dance-pop.md`. Paste everything inside the block into the
Lyrics box. Words are exactly as in `lyrics.md`, including the spelling, so they still match
what is sung on the source. Only the section tags are new.

Voice roles:

- **Soprano** = TAYLOR voice: soft, breathy, high.
- **Alto** = MILEY voice: husky, raspy, low.
- **Both** = together in harmony: soprano on top, alto underneath.

Shape: soprano opens the story, alto answers, they meet on every chorus, trade lines in the
dance break and "turn away, turn around", and end together.
