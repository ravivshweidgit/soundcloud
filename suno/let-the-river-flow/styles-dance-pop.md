# Let the river flow — modern dance styles (US market)

**Lead direction: E-rubber (MILEY voice) — WORKING, strongest so far.** Synth-pop at 100 BPM, Suno Cover.
Rubbery filter-envelope mono bass that drives the song and changes rhythm by section, husky
raspy alto (Miley Cyrus tone), warm filter-swept pads, funk guitar chops instead of arpeggios.

**Best Taylor version: E1 (TAYLOR voice) — WORKING.** Clean muted guitar riff, no arpeggios,
pads as sustained harmony only. The guitar beat the synth stabs by ear, same as in E-rubber.

**Also working: E2 (TAYLOR voice)** — same, with synth chord stabs instead of the guitar.

**Base: E (TAYLOR voice).** The base synth-pop style (Taylor Swift 1989 / Midnights sound) that
E-rubber grew from. For plain E with the working voice, swap its vocal sentence for:
`Husky raspy female alto lead vocal, smoky low chest voice, cool confident verses, gritty
belted chorus, clear American English diction, doubled vocals on the hooks.`

Gaga-style D needed 120 BPM, which rushed the vocal.

Each block pastes straight into the STYLES box. No artist names: Suno rejects or ignores them,

Lyrics box: `lyrics.md`. For **E-harmony (MILEY lead + TAYLOR harmony)**, use
`lyrics-harmony.md`. (`lyrics-duet.md` belongs to the rejected E-trade.)

```

Loud glossy radio mix, heavy sidechain. Dark but euphoric, dramatic, empowering, dance floor anthem. Avoid: chill, lo-fi, tropical, acoustic, ballad, trap, rap, rock guitars.
```

654 characters.

The tempo problem: the source is 100 BPM, and this style needs about 120. In Cover mode Suno
mostly keeps the source tempo, so at 100 this style tends to sound heavy and sluggish. Two ways:

1. Try it as is. Suno sometimes follows the Styles BPM and speeds the song up.
2. Speed the source up to 120 BPM before uploading, pitch unchanged (ratio 1.2):
   `ffmpeg -i original.wav -filter:a "atempo=1.2" original-120bpm.wav`
   The vocal will be faster but in the same key, and Suno then covers a song that is already
   at dance tempo. This is the reliable route.

Rejected: the melody only works at its natural speed. Sped up, the singing is rushed.

## If a take comes out wrong

Edit the Styles text, keep it under 1000 characters.

- Sounds like EDM festival, too hard: add `radio dance-pop, soft drop, no big supersaw drop`
- No drop / hook weak: add `catchy chopped vocal hook after every chorus`
- Too chill, not danceable: raise BPM by 4, add `driving four-on-the-floor, energetic`
- Accent not American: repeat `clear American English pronunciation`
- Grammar "fixed" or words changed: add `sing the lyrics exactly as written`
- Vocal buried under synths: add `lead vocal upfront, dry and clear`
- Too dark: switch `minor key` to `major key`, add `bright, euphoric`
- Too long intro: add `vocals start within 10 seconds`
- Tropical take feels sleepy at 100: add `bouncy groove, punchy kick` before touching the BPM
- Tempo drifts from the remix source: repeat `steady 100 BPM` at the start and end of the Styles text
