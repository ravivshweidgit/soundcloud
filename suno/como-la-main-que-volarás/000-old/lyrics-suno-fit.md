## Syllable match (why it fits)

`que alzarás` works because `que` + `al` merge into one sung syllable — that is what keeps
the title line at 8, exactly like `que volarás`.

## Optional swap

The original line ended on a **stressed** syllable (`cololindi-ÁL`). If the melody puts a
strong beat on that last note, this alternative lands better and is still 7 syllables:

Keep `en la orilla` if you prefer the river imagery over the stress match.

## English sense (not for pasting)

- Verse 1: You left a shadow in front of what you want / if it is as clear as water / the river goes on, but you are not here / if today you are at the shore
- Chorus: Like the hand you will raise / you will rise if you wait for it / you do not need wings / you do not need more
- Verse 2: In the evening or at dawn / your path is decided today / with today's life you will find yourself / if today you are at the shore
- Verse 3: In case you ever endure / no longer, no, no, you do not care for me / the river goes on without you / if today you are at the shore
