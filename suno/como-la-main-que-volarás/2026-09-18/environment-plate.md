# Iberian river valley — environment plate prompts

Two plates, one geography. Paste the blocks below the lines.

## Why two plates and not one

The light is the story. The film runs from blue pre-dawn at 0:00 to amber
sunrise at 2:52, and that warming is what makes it a "move on" video instead of
a "waiting" video. A single reusable plate at golden hour hands every scene the
same mood and the arc disappears. So the geography block below is identical in
both plates and only the light block changes.

Render **Plate A (cold)** for 0:00-1:12 and **Plate B (warm)** for 1:33-2:52.
1:12-1:33 is the crossover — the rising palm — so try it against both.

## What changed from the first version

- **The two banks are now built into the terrain.** Bare pale limestone shelf on
  one side, dense tall reeds on the other. The man's territory and the woman's
  territory are geographic facts, not prompt instructions. This is what keeps
  them apart in the footage.
- **The limestone bank stays cool even in the warm plate.** His ground never
  warms up. Only hers does.
- **No crossing of any kind.** No bridge, ford, stepping stones or jetty. Given
  one, the model will eventually use it.
- **The dirt track exists**, because 1:33 needs it — footprints stopping short.
- **Light moved out of the geography block** so it can be swapped cleanly.

Render each plate at 16:9 and 9:16.

## Plate A — cold, pre-dawn — APPROVED

Delivered and accepted. Limestone shelf on the left with no vegetation on it,
reed bank and dirt track on the right, river between. Plate B must match this
frame.

Create one reusable empty environment plate of a wide dry Iberian river valley.

GEOGRAPHY
One bank is a bare open shelf of weathered pale grey limestone, flat, swept
clean and hard, with no vegetation growing on it. The opposite bank is dense
with tall golden reeds standing higher than a person, backed by dry olive grass
and low scrub. Between the two banks a shallow river runs steadily over smooth
pebbles, continuous and unbroken from foreground to far distance. A narrow dirt
track runs along the reed bank and fades out into the grass. Distant sun-baked
limestone crags and dry hills close the valley behind, under soft atmospheric
haze.

MATERIALS
Rough weathered limestone, dry yellow grass, tall seeded reeds, smooth wet
river pebbles, fine dust, dew on the vegetation.

LIGHT
Cold blue pre-dawn before sunrise. No direct sun, the sun has not yet cleared
the hills. Flat soft shadowless ambient light, low contrast, desaturated
blue-grey palette, pale cool sky.

FINISH
Filmic live-action photography, natural light, organic 35mm film grain, matte,
muted. Not illustration, not painting, not 3D render.

DO NOT INCLUDE
No people, figures, body parts, faces, or character silhouettes. No bridge,
ford, stepping stones, jetty, boat, or any way of crossing the water. No
buildings, ruins, walls, fences, roads, vehicles, power lines, signage or
text. No animals. No watermark.

## Plate B — warm, sunrise

**Attach the approved Plate A as a reference image if the tool allows it.** A
text-only pass will invent a different valley, and the two plates have to be the
same place. The geography below is written to match the approved frame.

Where the sun is matters. It rises behind the rocky ridge on the **left**, so
the limestone shelf lies in that ridge's own shadow and stays cold, while the
light rakes across the valley and catches the reeds on the right. That is how
the warm side and the cold side coexist honestly in one frame.

Create one reusable empty environment plate of a wide dry Iberian river valley,
matching the attached reference frame exactly.

GEOGRAPHY
Same valley and same camera position as the reference. On the left, a bare open
shelf of weathered pale grey limestone, flat, swept clean and hard, stepping
down to the water in low ledges, with no vegetation growing on it. On the right,
a dense bank of tall golden reeds standing higher than a person, with a narrow
dirt track running along behind them and fading into dry grass. Between them a
shallow river runs steadily over smooth pebbles, entering at the bottom left of
frame and curving away into the middle distance. Layered ridges of sun-baked
limestone crags and dry hills close the valley behind, under soft atmospheric
haze.

MATERIALS
Rough weathered limestone, dry yellow grass, tall seeded reeds, smooth wet
river pebbles, fine dust, dew on the vegetation.

LIGHT
Low amber sunrise. The sun has just cleared the rocky ridge on the left and
rakes low across the valley. The reeds on the right are lit from behind and
glow gold, rimmed and translucent. Warm light catches the far hills and breaks
in gold across the surface of the water. The limestone shelf on the left lies in
the shadow of its own ridge and stays cool grey-blue, untouched by direct sun.
Warm right side, cold left side, in the same frame. Long shadows, soft warm
haze, higher contrast than pre-dawn.

FINISH
Filmic live-action photography, natural light, organic 35mm film grain, matte,
muted. Not illustration, not painting, not 3D render.

DO NOT INCLUDE
No people, figures, body parts, faces, or character silhouettes. No bridge,
ford, stepping stones, jetty, boat, or any way of crossing the water. No
buildings, ruins, walls, fences, roads, vehicles, power lines, signage or
text. No animals. No watermark. Do not let the sun fall directly on the
limestone bank. Do not move the camera or change the shape of the valley.
