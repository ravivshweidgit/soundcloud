## Translator's notes

- **alzarás** is future tense — "the hand you *will* raise". The whole song sits in the
  future and the conditional: nothing has happened yet, it is all what could happen if
  you wait.
- **te elevarás** is reflexive: you lift *yourself*. Not "you will be lifted". That is the
  point of the chorus — no wings, no rescue, you rise on your own.
- **la orilla** is the shore or riverbank. It carries the sense of standing at an edge
  without crossing, which is why the river keeps returning as the thing that moves while
  you do not.
- **Por si alguna vez aguantas** is deliberately unfinished in Spanish too — "in case you
  ever hold on..." with no consequence stated. Keep that hanging quality in English.
- **Dejaste sombra** (not *una sombra*) is poetic mass usage, closer to "you left shadow"
  than "you left a shadow". I used the article in English because it reads as broken
  otherwise.
