# Scene-level fixes

Regenerate only the broken scenes. Paste each block into that scene's prompt.

## What a scene prompt can and cannot fix

| Problem | Fixable per scene? |
|---|---|
| Man on a plinth, centred, too close | yes |
| Two men / duplicate figure | yes |
| Man present in the final section | yes |
| Camera moving when it should be locked | yes |
| Grey rolled-sleeve shirt | **no** — comes from the Character Bible |
| White bars at the edges | **no** — comes from the square plate / ratio |

## Scene at 0:52-1:12 — man on a pedestal

Was: centred on a raised block of stone, mid-size in frame, heroic staging.
Rising is the woman's arc. He belongs flat on the ground.

Extreme wide shot. One man alone, very small in the frame, standing flat on the open pale limestone shelf at river level on the left bank, placed off-centre toward the left edge. Long open charcoal overcoat to the knee, dark trousers, dark boots, sleeves to the wrist, hands empty at his sides. He is completely motionless. Locked static camera, no movement, no push in, no pull out. Across the shallow river on the right bank, tall golden reeds. Cold blue pre-dawn light, the limestone in shade, one long soft shadow. The landscape fills the frame edge to edge. Only one person in this shot. He is not standing on a rock, boulder, plinth, platform or any raised surface. No close-up, no mid-shot, he is never near the camera.

## Scene at 2:31-2:52 — the ending

Was: the man present, in close-up, duplicated near and far. This section is the
payoff for `Sigue el río sin ti` and must be empty of people.

Empty landscape. No people anywhere in this shot. A slow static view of the bare pale limestone shelf on the left bank, completely empty, the ground where someone stood earlier. Then the shallow river still running over smooth pebbles. Low amber sunrise catching the golden reeds on the far bank, while the limestone stays in cool grey shade. Soft haze, 35mm grain. The landscape fills the frame edge to edge. No person, no figure, no silhouette, no human shape, no shadow of a person anywhere in frame.

## Template for any other scene that breaks

Keep the same four parts in this order. It is the order that survives.

```
[SHOT SIZE + who is in it, or "no people anywhere in this shot"]
[wardrobe and hands, if a person]
[camera: locked, or drifting, or travelling]
[location and light]
The landscape fills the frame edge to edge.
[the specific thing that went wrong last time, stated as a negative]
```

Put the failure last and name it concretely. "He is not standing on a rock"
works; "avoid heroic framing" does not.
