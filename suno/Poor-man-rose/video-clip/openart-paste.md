## PLATE T — thaw · Step 2b · GREEN, NO FLOWERS

**Attach `plate-winter.png` as the reference — that one only.** The thaw is the winter coast
melting, so it has to come from the winter frame, not from the summer one. Using the fireweed
plate here would drag blooms back in.

**The rule for this plate: green arrives, flowers do not.** Fireweed is a high-summer bloom, so
spring in the north is grass, shoots, leaves and meltwater with no colour but green. It is also
what the verse asks for — *grow*, not bloom.

Create one empty sub-arctic spring landscape plate of the same coastline as @image1, wide 16:9, no animals and no people.

Same viewpoint, same coast, same headland on the right, same distant mountain range — now in early thaw. The snow has broken up into **ragged patches in the hollows and the lee of the rocks**, and the ground between them is wet and coming alive: **new green grass and low green shoots**, bright moss on the rocks, dark saturated soil. **Last year's dead fireweed stalks still standing** among the new growth, black, thin and bowed — the old year and the new year in the same frame. Meltwater everywhere: braided rivulets running down to the shore, shallow pools holding the sky. The sea is open, dark and cold, the shore ice gone or broken into last floes. The distant range still white on the summits, bare on the flanks. Soft grey overcast spring daylight, high thin cloud, clean cold air.

**The only colour in the frame is green** — new green, wet green, moss green, against grey rock, black stalks and dark water. Muted, not lush. Full colour photograph, cool and restrained.

Filmic live-action photography, natural light, organic 35mm film grain, matte.

Do not include: **any flowers, any buds, any magenta, pink or violet**, lupine, fireweed in bloom, full summer foliage, warm golden sunset light, deep snow cover, people, birds, animals, buildings, roads, cars, boats, text, watermark, black-and-white or monochrome rendering, illustration or painting look.

## PLATE L — lupine + snow peaks + double rainbow · Step 4 · MOST IMPORTANT

**Attach two references:**
- `../Album-cover/cover-referenc-09.jpg` — **primary: composition, snow peaks, calm lake, open sky**
- `../Album-cover/NEK_4965.webp` — **secondary: sky weather, storm cloud, double rainbow**

Take the layout and the mountains from the first reference and the sky and weather from the second.

Create one empty cinematic landscape plate, wide 16:9, no animals and no people.

LAYERS, bottom to top — keep these proportions:
- Lower third: a dense unbroken carpet of lupine flower spikes in violet, purple, magenta and pink, running continuously from the bottom edge back toward the water, no bare gaps or paths through it.
- A thin bright band of broad still lake water, mirror flat.
- Above that, a long range of high mountains with heavy snow lying on the summits, ridges and high gullies.
- The remaining upper half of the frame is SKY. The sky is the largest element in the picture — leave it open and uncluttered.

LIGHT — this geometry matters:
The low sun is OFF FRAME, behind the camera and slightly to one side. Its warm golden light rakes forward across the scene, lighting the tops of the lupine, skimming the lake, and igniting pink and gold alpenglow on the snow peaks. Long soft shadows stretch away from the camera and angle across the flower field. Do not show the sun itself.

SKY AHEAD:
A passing rain squall fills the sky in front of the camera — dark bruised blue-grey storm cloud, rain haze trailing over the far end of the lake. Across that dark cloud, a double rainbow: one bright full arc with a fainter second arc outside it, feet landing in the lake. The still water reflects the arc. Strong contrast between the warmly lit ground and the dark sky behind it.

Rich saturated colour. Epic, wide, immersive.

Filmic live-action photography, natural light, organic 35mm film grain, matte.

Do not include: people, birds, animals, text, watermark, buildings, roads, cars, boats, fireweed or roses instead of lupine, snowless bare brown peaks, the sun visible in frame, clear cloudless sunset sky, a rainbow in a clear sky with no rain or dark cloud, single pale rainbow, cartoon or sticker-like rainbow, flowers filling more than the lower third, tropical light, illustration or painting look.
