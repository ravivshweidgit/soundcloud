# Duluti Lake View Lodge — 30-second ad

**Goal:** make viewers want to spend the night at the lodge **before and after** their safari.
**Story:** fly in → lodge (rest before safari) → short safari burst → back to the lodge (unwind after) → contact.
**Viki in every shot.** No voice-over, no on-screen taglines. Music in the background, contact details on the end card only.

> Not to be confused with **Lake Duluti Lodge** (lakedulutilodge.com, the coffee-farm lodge) — a different
> property on the same lake. Its contacts must not appear in this ad.

Screen time: lodge **22 s**, safari **5 s**, flight **3 s**.

---

## Shots

### 1. Plane window — 0:00–0:03 (3 s)
Commercial airliner, window seat. Viki in the day safari outfit looks out of a standard cabin window.
Over her shoulder, Kilimanjaro's snow cap rises above the clouds in morning light.

### 2. Arrival at the lodge — 0:03–0:06 (3 s)
Over Viki's shoulder in the open safari jeep, rolling along a green tree-lined dirt road.
The jeep slows at the lodge's entrance wall: a fence of wooden poles with painted green leaves on top,
white letters **DULUTI ~ LAKE ~ VIEW** and gold **LODGE** beneath, purple canoes and painted fish below.

### 3. Morning coffee — 0:06–0:09 (3 s)
Restaurant terrace on the edge of the crater lake. A waitress sets a cup of coffee in front of Viki;
she lifts it and looks out over the calm green water.

### 4. Canoe — 0:09–0:12 (3 s)
Viki paddles a canoe on Lake Duluti. Birds skim the water, forested crater walls,
Mount Meru rising behind. Warm sunlight.

### 5. Safari — 0:12–0:15 (3 s)
Serengeti. Viki stands up in the open safari jeep with binoculars, scarf moving in the wind.
Zebra and giraffe close by.

### 6. Safari wide — 0:15–0:17 (2 s)
High wide shot: the open jeep crossing the golden plain trailing dust, animals all around,
a hot air balloon low in the sky behind. Viki small in the jeep.

### 7. Back to the lake — 0:17–0:20 (3 s)
Drone sweeps over Lake Duluti and the forest, Mount Meru behind, late golden light.
Viki small below, walking along the shore toward the trees.

### 8. Tree path — 0:20–0:23 (3 s)
Back shot: Viki walks the narrow tree-lined path toward her chalet, the lake glinting through
the trees on the right.

### 9. Sunset on the deck — 0:23–0:26 (3 s)
Viki in the evening dress on her chalet deck, glass of red wine, watching the sun go down.
Sky gold and orange, the forest darkening.

### 10. End card — 0:26–0:30 (4 s)
Hold on the sunset (Viki soft in frame or the empty deck). Contact only, two lines:

> **Duluti Lake View Lodge** · Arusha, Tanzania
> **[website or booking link]** · **[phone / WhatsApp]**

Contacts to get from the lodge — none found online. Prefer website (or Booking.com page) + WhatsApp number.
