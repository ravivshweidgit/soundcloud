# Safari — lyrics (Swahili)

A Tanzanian take on the Finnish verse from `../../harvoin/lyrics.md`: same shape, same feeling
(rarely we meet, in this far land, I wait by the water, always), moved from the north to Kilimanjaro.

## Paste into the LYRICS box

```
[Intro]
[distant female humming]
Mmm... ooh...

[Verse]
[soft female lead]
Mara chache tunakutana,
Tunapata kuonana
Katika nchi hii ya mbali,
Chini ya Kilimanjaro.

[Chorus]
[female lead, low male choir answering]
Tanzania,
Nchi ya jua
Nakungoja ziwani
Nakungoja daima

[Outro]
[choir humming, fading into the wind]
Nakungoja daima...
Mmm...
```

## Meaning

| Swahili | English | Finnish original |
|---|---|---|
| Mara chache tunakutana | Rarely do we meet | Harvoin yhtehen yhymme |
| Tunapata kuonana | We get to see each other | Saamme toinen toisihimme |
| Katika nchi hii ya mbali | In this faraway land | Näillä raukoilla rajoilla |
| Chini ya Kilimanjaro | Beneath Kilimanjaro | Poloisilla Pohjan mailla |
| Tanzania | Tanzania | Etelä-Pohjanmaa |
| Nchi ya jua | Land of the sun | Keskiyön auringon maa |
| Nakungoja ziwani | I wait for you by the lake | Odotan joella |
| Nakungoja daima | I wait for you always | Odotan aina |
