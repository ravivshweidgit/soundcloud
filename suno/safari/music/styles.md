## If a take comes out wrong

Edit the Styles text, keep it under 1000 characters.

- Too busy or too rhythmic: add `sparse percussion, ambient drone, no groove`
- Sounds like Afrobeats or dance: repeat `slow meditative, no beat`
- Words sound wrong or English: repeat `sung in Swahili, clear Swahili pronunciation`
- Voice too loud or too pop: add `soft distant vocals, no vibrato, no belting`
- Too generic spa music: add `African percussion, kalimba lead, East African choir`
