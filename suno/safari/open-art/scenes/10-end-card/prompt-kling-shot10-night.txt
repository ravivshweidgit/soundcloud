One continuous cinematic shot at blue hour, the camera almost still on the water with a very slow, gentle drift forward.

In the wooden dugout canoe, the man in the cap paddles calmly and steadily, the canoe gliding slowly forward towards the lit lodge. The woman in the olive dress sits relaxed in the front, gazing at the lodge. They stay seated the whole time.

On the warmly lit waterfront, the lodge team keeps waving goodbye with big happy waves, arms high, smiling. They stay in place on the waterfront.

The warm lodge lights glow and shimmer as long golden reflections on the calm water, gentle ripples spread from the paddle and the canoe. The trees stand still with a light breeze in the leaves. The deep blue sky stays clean and calm, a last soft glow of orange at the horizon.

No other boats or people. Photorealistic.

Sound: soft paddle strokes in the water, distant happy goodbye calls and laughter from the team, crickets and frogs at the lake at night. No music.
