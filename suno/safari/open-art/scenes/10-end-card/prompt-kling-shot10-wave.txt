One continuous cinematic shot, the camera almost still on the water with a very slow, gentle drift closer to the boat.

The friendly lodge team and the woman in the sun hat sit in the long wooden boat on the calm lake, all facing the camera, smiling and laughing. They wave goodbye to the camera with big happy waves, a few raise their hands high, and together they call out cheerfully: "See you at Duluti Lake View!" They stay seated in the boat the whole time; the boat drifts very slowly and rocks gently on the water. Everyone keeps the same faces and clothes.

Mount Meru stands behind them under a soft late-afternoon sky, purple jacaranda trees on the shore, a bird glides across the sky. Gentle ripples and reflections on the water in the foreground.

No other boats or people. Warm late-afternoon light. Photorealistic.

Sound: the group cheerfully calling "See you at Duluti Lake View!", laughter, soft water lapping on the boat, birds. No music.
