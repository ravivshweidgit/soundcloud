One continuous cinematic back shot. The camera follows slowly behind the woman at walking pace, smooth and steady like a gimbal, a few metres behind her.

The woman keeps walking naturally forward, away from the camera, along the leaf-covered forest path toward the wooden chalet with the green roof ahead. Relaxed steady stride the whole time; she never stops, never turns around and never steps back. Her red-blonde hair and the olive scarf over her shoulder move softly with her steps and the breeze.

On the right, the calm lake glints and shimmers gold through the gaps between the trees. Warm low sunbeams slant through the leaves, a few dry leaves drift down, dust glows in the light, branches sway gently overhead.

No other people or animals. Late golden afternoon light. Photorealistic.

Sound: her soft footsteps on dry leaves, birdsong, gentle breeze in the trees. No music, no speech.
