The ONE monitor lizard lies on the grassy bank under the trees at the far right edge of the lake, far from the woman. It is shy of people: it slips off the bank head first into the water with one small splash at the shoreline under the trees, dives under and disappears, leaving only the ripple rings seen in the last image. It never comes back up. There is only this one lizard in the whole shot, and that is the only splash: the rest of the lake stays calm, no fish jumping.

The woman takes a step, then pauses on the path and turns her head toward the distant splash.
The two small vervet monkeys on the path ahead of her stay near her, cheeky, one looking up at her hoping for food.
The grey heron glides across the lake from right to left with slow wingbeats, high above the water, its reflection following it.
The man in the wooden dugout canoe paddles slowly with a soft wake.
