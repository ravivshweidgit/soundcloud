# Safari — script

**Route:** Kilimanjaro → Serengeti.
One continuous flight: the camera leaves the plane and never cuts back inside.

---

## Opening

### 1. Plane window
Inside a commercial airliner, passenger cabin. Close on a standard rounded-rectangle cabin window
with the plastic shade half raised, morning light. The wing and clouds slide past below.
The camera drifts toward the glass.

### 2. Through the window — Kilimanjaro
The camera passes through the window into open air. Kilimanjaro rises out of the cloud sea,
snow cap lit by low sun, flat summit, glaciers on the rim.

### 3. Down to the Serengeti
The camera leaves Kilimanjaro behind and flies straight on, dropping lower. The land opens into
endless golden plains, scattered acacia trees, a long horizon.
The camera descends toward grass level.

### 4. Safari jeep, from far
Wide and high: a lone open safari jeep, no roof and open sides, driving across the plain and trailing dust.
Safari animals all around it: a zebra herd and wildebeest grazing nearby, giraffes among the acacias,
elephants and a few antelope in the distance.
Behind the jeep, in the background sky, a hot air balloon floats low over the plains.
The camera keeps its distance: small jeep, big land.

### 5. Arriving at Duluti Lake — inside the jeep
Point of view from inside the open jeep, looking forward past the driver's shoulder and the windscreen.
The jeep rolls slowly along a green, tree-lined dirt road; glimpses of Lake Duluti's water
through the forest. It pulls up to a wooden signboard that reads **DULUTI LAKE VIEW LODGE - ARUSHA**,
the lettering sharp and readable as the jeep stops in front of it.

### 6. Victoria Petrovski — coffee by the crater lake
Victoria Petrovski steps down from the open jeep and walks along the path to the lodge's
restaurant on the edge of the Duluti crater lake. She sits at a table facing the water; a waitress
brings her a cup of coffee. She holds the cup and looks out over the calm green lake,
forested crater walls rising all around it.

### 7. Canoe on Lake Duluti
Victoria walks down to the lakeshore, steps into a canoe and paddles out onto the calm water.
Birds fly low across the lake around her. On the tree-lined shore, vervet monkeys and blue monkeys
move through the branches. Mount Meru rises in the background above the crater rim.
Warm, golden sunlight over everything.

### 8. Drone flight — lake to lodge room
One continuous drone move, no cuts.
- **Rise:** the drone lifts off from just above Victoria's canoe and climbs high.
- **Overview:** a wide aerial view of the whole crater lake, forested walls all around,
  Mount Meru towering in the background, warm sunlight on the water.
- **Into the trees:** the drone turns and flies down into the eastern tree line beside the restaurant.
- **The path:** between the lake and the lodge room's balcony runs a narrow path, enclosed by trees.
  The drone drops low and stops over the path.
- **Victoria on the path:** Victoria walks into the path. Back shot: the drone follows behind her
  at shoulder height as she walks through the tree-surrounded path, the lake glinting through the
  trees on the right.
- **The balcony:** the drone rises ahead of her to the room's balcony and stops, hovering there.
- **Waiting:** the drone holds still on the balcony as Victoria climbs the stairs and arrives up
  onto the balcony.

### 9. Sundown on the balcony
Victoria enters the lodge room from the balcony. Inside, she changes into an elegant evening dress.
She steps back out onto the balcony with a glass of red wine and stands at the railing,
watching the sun go down over Lake Duluti. The water turns gold and orange, the forest darkens
around the crater, and Mount Meru is silhouetted against the evening sky.
