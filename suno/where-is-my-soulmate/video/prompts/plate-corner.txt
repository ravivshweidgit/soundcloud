Cinematic film still, 16:9, photographic realism, 35mm film grain, natural light. A harvested wheat field at the end of summer in the northern Negev, Israel, under a dark grey sky with no sun, flat dim light, colour drained. Short golden-grey stubble everywhere, except one corner of the field where a strip of tall standing wheat was left uncut on purpose — the corner (peah) left for the poor. In the stubble in the foreground lies one single forgotten bound sheaf of wheat on its side, alone. Nobody has come to take any of it. Camera low, the forgotten sheaf in the foreground left, the uncut corner standing behind on the right. No people, no machines, no text.
