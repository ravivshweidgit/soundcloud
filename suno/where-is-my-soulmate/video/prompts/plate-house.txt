Cinematic film still, 16:9, photographic realism, 35mm film grain, natural light. Months after a fire: a small single-storey kibbutz house in the western Negev, white plaster walls blackened with soot around the empty window and the doorway, the door gone, the doorframe charred. On the right side of the doorframe a small mezuzah case is still fixed, intact. In the garden in front, overgrown dry grass, a bougainvillea, and a simple children's swing hanging from a tree, moving slightly in the wind. Quiet overcast light, everything still. Nothing burning, no smoke, no debris piles, no blood. Camera at eye level from the garden, the doorway and the swing both in frame. No people, no text, no flags. Grief, ruin, a home.
