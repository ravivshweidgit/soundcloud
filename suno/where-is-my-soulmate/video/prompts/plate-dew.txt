Cinematic macro film still, 16:9, photographic realism, 35mm film grain. Extreme close-up of one red crown anemone petal at sunrise in a field in the northern Negev. On the edge of the petal hangs a single clear round drop of dew, and inside the drop the small bright rising sun is reflected, a tiny golden point of light. Soft warm golden backlight, the rest of the field a glowing red and gold blur. Hope. No people, no text.
