Cinematic film still, 16:9, photographic realism, 35mm film grain, natural light. Jerusalem, at the foot of the southern part of the Western Wall near Robinson's Arch, before dawn in soft blue-grey light: a heap of huge ancient Herodian limestone blocks lying broken and tumbled on the ancient paved street where they fell when the Romans threw them down from the Temple Mount in 70 CE. Massive pale stones with chiselled margins, cracked, half sunk into the broken paving, the tall ancient wall rising behind them out of frame. Camera low among the fallen stones. Silent, empty, no people, no signs, no fences, no text, no flags.
