Cinematic film still, 16:9, photographic realism, 35mm film grain, natural light. The end of summer in the western Negev, Israel, at dusk: a single tall white sea squill (Drimia maritima, hatzav) flower spike, about one metre high, densely covered with small white star flowers, standing completely alone in a wide bare dry field of pale loess and dry stubble. Nothing else grows near it. A thin yellow ribbon is tied around its stalk, its ends moving slightly in the wind. Deep blue and pale amber dusk sky, the sun already set, soft light on the white flowers. The squill is the only vertical in the frame, on the left third. Camera at the height of the flower, facing it as if facing a person. No people, no text. A memorial, loneliness.
