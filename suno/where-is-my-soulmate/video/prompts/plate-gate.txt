Cinematic film still, 16:9, photographic realism, 35mm film grain, natural light. Early morning at the end of summer at the edge of a small kibbutz in the western Negev, Israel. A straight pale dirt road comes from the far horizon across dry harvested fields toward the camera and ends at a closed yellow metal kibbutz entrance gate — a simple painted yellow swing gate of steel pipes, shut. Low cypress and eucalyptus trees behind the gate, the edge of red-tiled roofs barely visible far behind. The road is completely empty, a little dust hanging over it. Low warm sun from the side, long shadows across the road. Fixed camera, eye level, on the inside of the gate looking out along the road. No people, no vehicles, no text, no signs, no flags. Waiting, abandonment, silence.
