Cinematic film still, 16:9, photographic realism, 35mm film grain. Close view of the huge ancient pale limestone blocks of the Western Wall in Jerusalem before dawn, cold blue-grey light, the first faint warm light touching the top. Small tufts of caper plants grow from the joints. Many small folded paper notes are pushed into the cracks between the stones, folded tight, no writing visible on any of them. The wall fills the whole frame. Completely empty, no people, no hands, no signs, no flags, no text anywhere.
