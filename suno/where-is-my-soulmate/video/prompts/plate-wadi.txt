Cinematic film still, 16:9, photographic realism, 35mm film grain, natural light. A dry wadi riverbed in the western Negev like Nahal Besor at the end of summer: a wide winding bed of pale cracked mud plates, curled dry clay, no water at all, low loess banks with a few dry tamarisk and eucalyptus trees. Overcast grey-silver light, heavy clouds. The riverbed leads away from the camera to the horizon, empty. Camera low, at the edge of the cracked mud, wide lens, deep focus. No people, no animals, no text. Bereavement, dryness, endless absence.
