# שדות החורף — shot list

Concept: `clip-concept.md`. Every prompt inherits its style base and hard rules.
Times come from `timing.json` / `timing-tail.json` (Whisper word timestamps on the final take).
Times marked `~` are estimates; confirm against the audio before the edit.

**One clip per sung line.** Lines run about 4 s in the verses, but only about 2 s at the end
of wheat 2 — those shots are cut short in the edit, not generated short.

**Pairs across the year** — build each pair from one base still, re-lit, never from scratch:
3↔22↔45 (anemone field), 8↔25↔48 (wadi), 9↔34 (wall), 10↔35 (hourglass), 11↔36 (road),
12↔13↔37↔38 (sunrise frame), 14↔31 (her at the edge), 15↔26↔39 (corner and sheaf),
28↔29↔30 (squill), 17–21↔40–44 (wheat 1 and wheat 2), 2↔18↔41 (the crack).

## Intro — 0:00–0:18 · before dawn, end of summer · gold

0. **0:00** — **The observation room before dawn.** Dark, only the glow of a wall of monitors.
   The observer from behind: plain uniform, no insignia, hair tied back, headset. On her screens,
   in the surveillance look, the quiet fields. She watches. Slow push in past her shoulder into
   one screen until it fills the frame and we are in the fields. **Exactly the frame of shot 54**
   — same room, same angle; at the start she is in the chair, at the end it is empty.
1. **~0:05** — Black, slowly opening onto a harvested wheat field before dawn, deep blue going
   gold. The hourglass stands in the stubble, whole, sand running. Static.
2. **~0:09** — Low over the dry earth: a thin crack already runs through the loess. The rift
   was there before the morning.

## Chorus 1 — 0:18–0:34 · the after, seen first · winter red

3. **0:17.9 `על שְׂדוֹת החורף`** — The anemone field, low at flower height, storm sky with a
   silver break. Slow push in. *Base still: `plates/plate-kalaniyot`.*
4. **~0:20 `דם כלניות נשפך`** — Close: dense anemones moving in the wind. The lives.
5. **~0:22 `בין פרגים נפרח`** — One poppy alone, close, closing its petals. The dead.
6. **0:25.8 `על שְׂדוֹת החורף`** — Wider field, the low mud wall crossing it.
7. **~0:28 `דם כלניות נשפך`** — Wind passes over the red like a wave.
8. **~0:30 `נחלי אכזב`** — The dry cracked wadi. The woman, from behind, walks away along it.

## Verse 1 — 0:35–1:08 · that morning

9. **0:35.6 `איך התנפצו חומות חֵמָר`** — **Frontal, static.** The mud wall; the crack opens and
   the wall breaks apart. No softening.
10. **~0:39.5 `לָמָּה שעון החול נפרץ`** — **Frontal, static.** The hourglass from shot 1, now at
    first light. The glass cracks; sand pours out over the stubble.
11. **~0:43.4 `אין איש לא בא, אף לא אחד`** — **The army that did not come.** A dirt road from
    the horizon to a closed yellow kibbutz gate, fixed camera. Shadows swing as hours pass in
    seconds. Nothing comes; nobody opens the gate from outside. Dust settles.
12. **0:47.3 `איפה הבוקר שעלה`** — A beautiful sunrise over the field, dew, birds lifting.
    A forgotten scarf caught on a thorn bush moves in the morning wind.
13. **0:51.4 `איפה השמש נאלמה`** — **Hard cut on the word, no transition — the shock.** Same
    frame, now a dark grey day: a colossal wall of black smoke fills the whole sky and hides the
    horizon, no sun, no light. Nothing moves; the scarf hangs limp; only the smoke rolls slowly.
    *Base still: `plates/plate-sunrise-dark.png`.*
14. **0:55.8 `איפה אמצא אוֹתְךָ עכשיו`** — The woman at the field's edge, back to us, facing the
    horizon where the black smoke hangs. Only her hair and sweater move.
15. **1:00.1 `לקט פֵּאָה ושיכחה`** — Grey harvested field: the uncut corner standing, one
    forgotten sheaf on the ground. Nobody comes for it.
16. **1:04.2 `רק תן לי טיפות טיפות של אור`** — One thin ray breaks through the smoke and falls
    on a single anemone. The first light since 13.

## Wheat 1 (`[Bridge]`) — 1:08–~1:24 · winter to spring

17. **1:08.4 `כמו גלי חיטה`** — A sea of wheat ears to the horizon. A gust of wind runs
    through it and strikes waves — dark and light bands rolling across the field like swell
    on water, one after another. Wide, low, camera static so only the wheat moves. Poppies
    among the ears.
18. **~1:12 `אם רק אוכל לצמוח שוב`** — **Growth out of the break, hope.** One green wheat shoot
    pushes up out of the crack itself — the same crack in the loess as shots 2 and 9.
19. **~1:16 `זורעים בדמעה`** — **We carry the break in our hearts, always.** The woman's hand
    (hand only) scattering seed along the crack. The crack stays open; the seed falls into it
    and around it. Sowing does not close the break — it is sown with it.
20. **1:18.0 `חֶרְמֵשׁ בקמה`** — **Death the reaper.** Close: the curved blade enters standing
    grain in one sweep and the ears fall, slowed. Never see who holds it — only the blade, so
    it reads as the reaper's sickle, not a farmer at work. Dark, cold light on the steel.
21. **~1:21 `מלאו חֲלָקוֹת בבר`** — Slow move over rows of golden sheaves, poppies among them.
    Full and abundant. *Lock this move exactly — it returns in 40.*

## Chorus 2 — ~1:24–1:40 · winter rain

22. **`על שְׂדוֹת החורף`** — The anemone field (3) in rain.
23. **`דם כלניות נשפך`** — Rain hitting the petals, close.
24. **`בין פרגים נפרח`** — The poppy, wet, closed.
25. **`... נחלי אכזב`** (to 1:39) — The wadi (8): rain falls and it stays dry.

## Instrumental — 1:40–1:56 · a year passes

25a. Time-lapse over one field: green → gold → harvest → stubble. End of summer again.

## Verse 2 — 1:56–2:56 · the year after

26. **1:56.6 `לקט פֵּאָה ושיכחה`** — The same corner (15), a year later, again not taken.
27. **2:01.3 `איפה הבית שחרב`** — A burned kibbutz house, after: black walls, empty window,
    a swing moving in the yard, **a mezuzah still on the burned doorframe**. Cut on the stones
    to the Herodian blocks thrown down in 70 CE, still lying at the foot of the Western Wall.
    Then one second of the Western Wall empty before dawn, no people, folded notes between the
    stones (unreadable). No Dome of the Rock, no Temple Mount skyline.
28. **2:05.5 `שוב בשדה עומד חָצָב`** — **A memorial to the personal loss — the beloved who did
    not come back.** One tall white squill spike standing alone in a wide, bare, dry field at
    dusk, nothing else growing near it. Upright like a person standing; the only vertical in
    the frame. Static, held, the camera at its height as if facing someone. A thin yellow ribbon
    is tied around its stalk — the only time the ribbon appears.
29. **2:09.9 `אֱמוֹר לָמָּה לא חזרת עוד`** — **Despair: the dead do not return, and she asks
    anyway.** The squill's field (28). The woman, back to us, stands facing the squill. No
    answer — only the wind. On the end of the line she sinks down to the ground beside it.
30. **~2:14 `עוד חורף בא ואתה לא`** — **The despair goes on; she says it to herself — another
    winter without you.** Same frame as 29: she sits on the ground beside the squill, back to us.
    The first rain of winter begins to fall on her and on the field. She does not move, does not
    take shelter. The bereavement carried always.
31. **2:18.1 `איפה אמצא אוֹתָךְ עכשיו`** — Shot 14's frame, green, no smoke. She is still there.
32. **~2:22 `מים רַבִּים לא יְכַבּוּ`** — **The feeling stays alive; the waters are the time
    that passes, and it does not let go.** A memorial candle in a glass on a stone in the field,
    rain falling on it. Time-lapse: clouds race, day and night pass over it, the rain keeps
    coming — the flame never goes out.
33. **2:26.7 `רק תן לי טיפות טיפות של אור`** — Rain drops on anemones catching the sun, glittering.
34. **2:31.6 `איך התנפצו חומות חֵמָר`** — Frontal, static: the wall a year after it fell (9) — the
    breach still open, the fallen bricks still lying in it among dry weeds, nobody rebuilt it.
    *Base still: `plates/plate-wall-ruin.png`, made from the last frame of 9.*
35. **2:35.3 `לָמָּה שעון החול נפרץ`** — **The empty inquiry table.** Frontal, static: a long
    official commission table standing alone in the open field among red poppies — microphones
    and water glasses, **no chairs at all, no name plates**. It accuses no one in person: the
    inquiry table itself stayed empty. On the table lie the shards of the hourglass and its
    spilled sand: the evidence, and nobody to examine it. To this day there has been no
    inquiry. Twin of the Shabbat table (46). *Base still: `plates/plate-commission.png`.*
36. **2:39.6 `אין איש לא בא`** — The road and the yellow gate (11), green now, still empty.
37. **2:43.4 `איפה הבוקר שעלה`** — The sunrise frame (12), a year later.
38. **2:47.6 `איפה השמש נאלמה`** — The same frame; the memory of smoke — a dark band still on
    the horizon. Stillness.
39. **2:52.0 `לקט פֵּאָה ושיכחה`** — The forgotten sheaf, close.

## Wheat 2 — 2:56–3:11 · the plots are the cemetery

40. **2:56.2 `כמו גלי חיטה`** — Shot 17 again: the wind striking waves through the sea of ears.
41. **2:59.7 `אם רק אוכל לצמוח שוב`** — The crack (18) a year on: a line of wheat now grows
    along it. The break is still there, under the green.
42. **3:04.0 `זורעים בדמעה`** — The hand sowing along the crack (19). ~2 s.
43. **3:06.1 `חֶרְמֵשׁ בקמה`** — The sickle (20); this time a poppy falls with the ears. ~2 s.
44. **3:08.2 `מלאו חֲלָקוֹת בבר`** — **Shot 21's exact move.** The sheaves give way to rows of
    plain gravestones in a kibbutz cemetery set in the fields. **The poppies stay in the same
    place in frame across the cut**; only what surrounds them changes. No names, no text.

## Chorus 3 — 3:11–3:28 · the field at its peak

45. **3:10.9 `על שְׂדוֹת החורף`** — The anemone field at its fullest, wide.
46. **~3:13 `דם כלניות נשפך`** — The empty Shabbat table set in the middle of the anemone
    field: white cloth in the wind, unlit candles, challah, every chair empty, one pulled out.
    *Base still: `plates/plate-table`.*
47. **3:15.2 `בין פרגים נפרח`** — Poppies among the gravestones, close.
48. **3:18.9–3:27.7** — The second half of the chorus: the field, the wall, the wadi (8 again).

## Chorus 4 — 3:28–3:45 · she goes in

49. **3:27.7–3:36** — The waiting woman walks into the anemone field, back to us, the red up to
    her waist. The observer is beside her — out of the screens for the first time, in the field
    with her. Both from behind. They walk hand in hand.
50. **3:36–3:44.8** — Wider, high, locked camera. They walk on together, and the observer is
    simply gone — she is not alive. The woman keeps walking with her arm still out to the side,
    her hand still closed around the hand that is no longer there. She does not let go. She
    grows smaller among the flowers until she too is gone into the field. On `נחלי אכזב`
    (3:40.7) the field is empty.
    *Technique: two stills, same frame — both women / the woman alone with her hand still
    held out (still edit). Kling clip from each, cut or short dissolve between them in ffmpeg.*

## Outro — 3:45–4:23 · 38 s, no singing · the sun returns

51. **3:45** — The empty anemone field before dawn. Hold.
52. **~4:00** — **The first clean sunrise of the film.** No smoke. Light crosses the red.
53. **~4:12** — Close: a drop of dew on an anemone holding the sun.
54. **~4:16–4:23** — **The reveal.** The observation room. Her chair is empty, the headset lies on
    the desk. On the screens: the anemone field in the clean sunrise, seen from above. She is still
    watching. Fade on the last note.

## Working notes (for whoever continues)

- Talk to the author in Hebrew. Only the Hebrew lyrics (`../lyrics-he-suno.txt`) matter.
- Read `clip-concept.md` first; its hard rules and locked decisions are not up for change.
- One folder per shot: `shots/<start s>_<shot no>_<description>/prompt.txt`, then
  `C:\Python313\python.exe tools/batch.py FOLDER=PLATE[=END_PLATE]` from `video/`. Stills:
  `tools/still.py`. Rejected takes and stills go to `notInUse/`, never deleted.
- Look at every `sheet.jpg` before placing a shot. Reject: faces, text, fire or orange glow in
  the smoke, purple stubble (fix with the shot-1 grade in `edit.json`), extra people.
- Every Kling request id is in `shots/requests.log`; an interrupted run can be recovered with
  `tools/clip.py --fetch ID OUT`. Check the folder before re-running — a paid clip may exist.
- Preview with `tools/assemble.py edit.json previews/NAME.mp4 --from S --to S`.
- Stronger model for: 13, 27 (house and Western Wall), 49–50 (the hand), 54, the final edit.
