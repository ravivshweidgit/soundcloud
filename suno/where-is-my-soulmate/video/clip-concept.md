# שדות החורף — video clip concept

Track: `../sdot-hachoref.mp3` — 4:23 (263.4 s)
Lyrics: `../lyrics-he-suno.txt` — the Hebrew is the only source. The English song plays no part.
Meanings: `../lyrics-he.md`, section "Meaning: October 7"
Shot list: `shot-list.md` · Timing: `timing.json`, `timing-tail.json`

## Logline

One year in the fields of the Gaza envelope, from the end of summer to the end of summer,
**seen through the eyes of a field observer (תצפיתנית).** At her screens she sees everything
that happens to the country: the wall breaking, the hourglass breached, the road where no one
comes, the morning that rises and the sun silenced. She keeps watching — the wheat that grows
again, the plots that filled up, the empty tables — and on her screens she watches a woman in
the fields who waits for the one who did not come back. Only at the end do we understand that
the observer is no longer alive: she watches the country she left behind from the sky.

## The two women

- **The observer** — the eyes of the film. A nameless figure, not any real observer; her death
  is never shown. Seen only from behind: plain uniform with no insignia, hair tied back,
  headset, a wall of monitors in a dark observation room.
- **The waiting woman** — alive, she stayed among the living. The sweater and olive skirt of
  `woman-lock.png`. Seen only from behind. The observer watches her on the screens.

## How the observer sees

- **Through the screens.** Some shots appear on her monitors in the surveillance look (green
  monochrome, scan lines, monitor curve, 8 fps stutter) — the hourglass repeats in 10, the road
  in 11, the waiting woman in verse 2. Surveillance cameras stand high on masts, so these views
  look down from above.
- **From the sky.** After 13 the high view takes over more and more: soft, silent, looking down
  on the fields, the cemetery, the tables. The viewer feels the gaze change before knowing why.
- **The reveal.** The last shot returns to the observation room: her chair empty, the headset
  on the desk, and on the screens the anemone field seen from above. She is still watching.
- **The frame.** The whole film sits inside her screen, but the screen is seen only at the
  edges. Shot 0 pushes into a monitor until it fills the frame. In verse 1 we return to the
  room only at the key moments (the hourglass repeats, the gate, and on 13 her screens go grey
  with smoke — her last moment in the room). From 13 the screen never comes back: the view is
  clean and high, from the sky. Shot 54 pulls out of the sunrise back onto the monitor and the
  empty chair. Shots 0 and 54 are the same frame: she is there at the start, gone at the end.

## Hard rules

- **No violence on screen.** No attackers, weapons, soldiers, fire, blood. Only the black smoke
  and what it takes. Burned houses are shown after, empty and still.
- **No real faces, names or readable text.** Gravestones without names. No flags.
- **Both women are seen only from behind or in silhouette.** Two figures, each with one
  consistent costume. They share a frame only once, at the end (shots 49–50).
- **No sound added to the song.**

## The year (the spine)

| Section | Season |
|---|---|
| Intro, chorus 1 | before dawn, end of summer → the winter anemones (the after, seen first) |
| Verse 1 | that morning: end of summer, Simchat Torah |
| Wheat 1 (`[Bridge]`) | winter to spring: green wheat, sowing, the sickle, full plots |
| Chorus 2 | winter rain on the anemones |
| Instrumental 99–116 s | time passes: harvest → end of summer again |
| Verse 2 | the year after: the same places, a year older, nothing answered |
| Wheat 2 | the same cycle — the plots are now the cemetery |
| Choruses 3–4, outro | the anemone field at its peak → the sun rises clean |

## Colour score

- Intro and verse 1 up to `השמש נאלמה`: warm end-of-summer gold.
- From `השמש נאלמה`: **black smoke covers the sun, the wind stops, colour drains to grey.**
- **The red of anemones and poppies is the only saturated colour** from then on.
- Light returns in steps only: one ray on `טיפות של אור` in verse 1, glittering rain drops on
  the same line in verse 2, and the **clean sunrise only in the outro.** The sun is life.

## Symbols (from the Hebrew, as the author defined them)

- **Anemones** — the lives that were murdered. Alive, dense, moving in wind.
- **Poppies** — the dead. Single, close, closing at evening. They grow among the wheat ears,
  and in wheat 2 among the gravestones — the one thing that stays in place across the cut.
- **`נחלי אכזב`** — bereavement. A dry cracked wadi (the Besor); the woman walks it alone.
  Every chorus ends there.
- **`חומות חֵמָר`** — the rift in Israeli society. A mud wall cracking, the crack splitting the
  field. It was already there before the morning — a thin crack is visible in the intro.
- **`שעון החול נפרץ`** — the field observers' radio that morning (Roni Eshel z"l, Nahal Oz);
  the code name of the fence sector. An hourglass in the field; the glass cracks, the sand runs out.
- **`איך` / `לָמָּה` together** — the questions of a commission of inquiry, a protest against
  the state and the establishment. **Frontal, static, unforgiving camera** on both lines, unlike
  the soft camera everywhere else. In verse 2 the wall is still cracked, and the shards of the
  hourglass lie on an **empty commission table standing among the poppies** — microphones and
  water, no chairs and no name plates, so it accuses no one in person: the inquiry table itself
  stayed empty. The evidence, and nobody to examine it: to this day there has been no inquiry.
  It mirrors the empty Shabbat table.
- **`אין איש לא בא`** — the army that did not come. An empty road; hours pass on the shadows.
- **`הבוקר שעלה`** — the morning that was lost, the loss of life. A beautiful sunrise.
- **`השמש נאלמה`** — with alef: struck silent by shock; heard as `נעלמה`: hidden, lost.
  Nova survivors could not see the sun for the smoke. **Black smoke swallows the sun; all
  motion stops.**
- **`אמצא אוֹתְךָ` / `אוֹתָךְ`** — the hostages and the dead who were not brought back.
  The woman at the field's edge facing the smoky horizon; in verse 2 the same frame, green.
- **`לקט פֵּאָה ושיכחה`** — the commandment to leave food for the weak; a protest that the weak
  were abandoned against it. An uncut corner and a forgotten sheaf that nobody comes to take.
- **`הבית שחרב`** — the Temple destroyed over baseless hatred, and the houses burned on
  October 7. A burned kibbutz house, a mezuzah still on the doorframe; cut to the Herodian
  stones the Romans threw down, still lying at the foot of the Western Wall.
- **`חָצָב`** — standing alone, a memorial to the personal loss: the beloved the song speaks to,
  who did not come back. Also the end of summer again — a year has passed.
- **`מים רַבִּים לא יְכַבּוּ`** — Song of Songs 8:7, "love strong as death". The feeling stays
  alive; the waters are the time that passes, and it does not let go. A memorial candle in the
  rain, days and nights passing over it, the flame never out.
- **`טיפות של אור`** — hope, expecting good.
- **Wheat** — growth and life (`גלי חיטה`); **`לצמוח שוב`** echoes "החיטה צומחת שוב"
  (Yom Kippur 1973, Beit HaShita); **sickle** — death reaping lives; **`מלאו חֲלָקוֹת בבר`** —
  "מלאו אסמינו בר" (Pinchas Lander, HaGivatron): the wheat plots, and the cemetery plots that
  filled up. `בר` is grain and wild flowers — people, who pass.

## Style base (paste into every prompt)

Cinematic film still / shot, photographic realism, 35mm film grain, natural light only. The
western Negev and Gaza envelope: open rolling fields, loess earth, eucalyptus lines on the
horizon, dry wadis, kibbutz edges. Low camera, wide lens, slow or static camera. Reverent,
quiet, grief held. No people unless the woman is specified; no text, no logos, no flags.

## The waiting woman

Late twenties, long dark wavy hair loose, plain oversized undyed wool sweater, long dark olive
skirt, no jewelry. **Always from behind or in silhouette; her face never appears.**
Lock still: `plates/woman-lock.png`.

## Locked decisions

1. No radio static. The song stays clean; the frontal static camera carries the accusation.
2. No rocket trails. Only the black smoke.
3. A forgotten scarf moves on a bush in the sunrise of `הבוקר שעלה`.
4. The empty road leads to a closed yellow kibbutz gate. Nobody opens it from outside.
5. A yellow ribbon tied to the squill, once only (shot 28).
6. The empty Shabbat table in the anemone field, chorus 3 (shot 46).
7. Ending: the observer, out of the screens for the first time, walks into the anemone field
   hand in hand with the waiting woman. The observer is simply gone, because she is not alive;
   the woman keeps holding her hand — the arm still out, the hand closed on nothing — and walks
   on into the field until she is gone from sight. The outro holds the field until the
   first clean sunrise, then the empty chair.
8. The observer wears a plain uniform with no insignia.

## Folders

- `plates/` — the approved base stills, shared by several shots (`plate-<name>.png`, `woman-lock.png`). Rejected stills in `plates/notInUse/`.
- `prompts/` — the prompts of the stills (`plate-<name>.txt`).
- `shots/<start seconds>_<shot number>_<description>/` — one folder per shot, e.g. `shots/038.90_10_hourglass-breached/`:
  the clip `<folder name>.mp4`, its `prompt.txt`, `sheet.jpg` (six frames), `log.txt`, and older takes in `notInUse/`.
- `shots/requests.log` — every paid Kling request id.
- `edit.json` — the edit: shot start times on the song, clip, offset into the clip, colour grade.
- `previews/` — assembled sections on the master audio.

## Tools (`tools/`, run from `video/`)

- `still.py` — stills with Nano Banana Pro: `python tools/still.py prompts/plate-x.txt plates/plate-x.png --ref ... --n 2`
- `batch.py` — generate shots in parallel: `python tools/batch.py 038.90_10_hourglass-breached=plate-hourglass [...]`.
  Reads the shot's `prompt.txt`, moves an existing take to `notInUse/vN.mp4`, writes the clip and `sheet.jpg`.
- `clip.py` — one Kling 3.0 Pro image-to-video request on fal, no audio; `--fetch REQUEST_ID out.mp4` re-downloads a paid clip.
- `assemble.py` — cut the shots of `edit.json` to their times over the master audio: `python tools/assemble.py edit.json previews/x.mp4 --from 34.8 --to 68.43`.
- `contact.py` — contact sheet of `-1`/`-2` plate pairs.
- `transcribe.py` — Whisper word timing of the take.
