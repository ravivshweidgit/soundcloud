## C. Shir's voice (light mezzo, clear tone)

Paste from `styles-he-shir-suno.txt`. Three Shir-voice styles: (1) intimate cinematic ballad, (2) cinematic philharmonic, (3) huge philharmonic strings in front. Same vocal in all three; only the arrangement changes. Vocal notes (from her TikTok clip):

- Light mezzo that lives around C4 to G♯4 and rarely drops below G3, so no deep chest voice.
- Clean, clear tone, less breathy than the Suno guide vocal.
- Moderate vibrato on held notes, about 5 Hz.
- Narrow loud-soft range: she builds with colour, not belting, so the final chorus is a bright mixed voice and the top lines are head voice.

Use the A minor (+2) source so the verse lows land on her G3 floor. The intro and outro still reach D6, which is why the top lines are asked for in head voice. Generate a few takes and keep the one where the verses sound closest to her; Style Influence a little higher than usual helps Suno drop the source singer's timbre.

## Cover checklist

- Upload `where-is-my-soulmate.mp3` and choose Cover.
- Lyrics box: only the block under "Suno lyrics" in `lyrics-he.md`.
- Leave out the key. Suno keeps the source key; asking for another one makes the vocal pitchy.
- Every line is in a new language, so Suno has more room to re-melodise. If the melody wanders, raise Audio Influence in More Options.
- If it still sounds like the old arrangement, do the opposite: lower Audio Influence, raise Style Influence.
- Hebrew mispronounced: compare against the niqqud version in `lyrics-he.md`, and respell the word in the lyrics box the way it should sound.
