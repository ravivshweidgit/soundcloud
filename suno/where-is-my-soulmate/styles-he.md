# Where is my soulmate — Hebrew cover styles

Source upload: `where-is-my-soulmate.mp3` (about 112 BPM). Lyrics box: the block in `lyrics-he.md`.

## A. Israeli memorial ballad, female lead

Paste into the STYLES box:

```
Israeli memorial ballad cover, sung in Hebrew, steady 112 BPM with a slow half-time feel, 4/4, keep the original melody, phrasing and vocal speed. Raw emotional female lead vocal, warm low chest voice in the verses, voice cracking and breaking with grief on the hardest lines, soaring belted final chorus, clear native Israeli Hebrew diction. Soft felt piano, warm cello and string ensemble, sustained pads, soft brushed drums entering at the first chorus, timpani and full strings building into the last chorus. Spacious intimate mix, long hall reverb. Grief, longing, remembrance, quiet hope. Avoid: EDM, trap, rap, dance beat, upbeat, autotune, American accent.
```

664 characters.

Half-time feel: the source tempo stays at 112 so the melody keeps its grid, while the drums move at half speed so it sounds like a ballad.

## B. Re-cover of a good take with new lyrics

Upload the good take as the source and paste `styles-he-recover-suno.txt`. It opens with a lock sentence, then repeats style A so Suno rebuilds the same sound. Set Audio Influence high and Style Influence low.

## Cover checklist

- Upload `where-is-my-soulmate.mp3` and choose Cover.
- Lyrics box: only the block under "Suno lyrics" in `lyrics-he.md`.
- Leave out the key. Suno keeps the source key; asking for another one makes the vocal pitchy.
- Every line is in a new language, so Suno has more room to re-melodise. If the melody wanders, raise Audio Influence in More Options.
- If it still sounds like the old arrangement, do the opposite: lower Audio Influence, raise Style Influence.
- Hebrew mispronounced: compare against the niqqud version in `lyrics-he.md`, and respell the word in the lyrics box the way it should sound.
