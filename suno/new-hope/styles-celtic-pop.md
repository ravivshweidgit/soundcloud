## If a take comes out wrong

The third-voice notes only apply to the three-voice version above.

Edit the Styles text, keep it under 1000 characters.

- Third voice sings words: add `wordless third voice, vowels only, no lyrics`
- Third voice missing: add `continuous high wordless ooh pad above the harmony`
- Third voice too loud: add `distant airy pad far behind the two singers`
- Sounds like a choir: strengthen `three singers only, no choir, no stacked vocals`
- Too traditional, not modern: add `contemporary pop production, punchy drums`
- Too pop, Celtic lost: add `tin whistle and fiddle in the foreground, modal melody`
- Choruses not big enough: add `wide anthemic chorus, doubled vocals, soaring whistle`
- Words unclear, voices blur: add `lead voice clearly forward, harmony quieter underneath`
- Harmony voice too buried: add `audible second harmony voice, equal blend`
- Drums too heavy for the verses: add `verses almost no drums, drums enter on the chorus`
- Too fast: lower to 88 BPM. Too sleepy: raise to 104 BPM
- Fake Gaelic words: repeat `sung in English, clear English diction`
