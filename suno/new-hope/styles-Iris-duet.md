# New Hope — Irish Celtic style, duet

Two female voices. For the single-voice version see `styles-Irish.md`.

## Paste into the STYLES box

```
Traditional Irish Celtic folk ballad, sung in English, 76 BPM, 4/4, Dorian modal melody. Two female Celtic folk voices, warm dreamy delivery, Irish lilt, traditional ornamentation with grace notes, cuts and rolls on the lead. Lead voice alone on the verses, second voice joining in close two-part harmony a third below on the choruses, intimate and blended, two singers in one room. Celtic harp arpeggios, tin whistle and low whistle answering the vocal lines, lyrical fiddle, DADGAD acoustic guitar, soft bodhran heartbeat, low uilleann pipe drone underneath. Sparse harp and guitar on the verses, whistle countermelody and harp sparkle on the choruses, fiddle lead on the bridge, low whistle and fading drone on the outro. Hopeful, morning light, natural stone-chapel reverb, organic and elegant. Avoid: choir, stacked vocals, pop belting, autotune, jig, reel, drum machine, synths, trap.
```

890 characters.

Lyrics box: paste the block in `lyrics-suno.md`. The lyrics further down this file are the earlier Suno submission, kept as a record.

## If a take comes out wrong

Edit the Styles text, keep it under 1000 characters.

- Sounds like a choir: strengthen `two singers only, duet, no choir, no stacked vocals`
- Harmony disappears: add `clear second harmony voice, audible two-part harmony`
- Second voice too loud: add `lead voice forward, harmony voice quieter underneath`
- Harmony on every line: add `harmony only on the choruses, verses solo`
- Too generic folk, not Irish: add `modal melody, Mixolydian, pipe drone`
- Too fast or too bouncy: add `slow ballad, no dance rhythm`, lower to 70 BPM
- Fake Gaelic words: repeat `sung in English, clear English diction`
- Voices too pop: add `traditional folk singers, no vibrato, no belting`

The `Avoid:` tail is there because this Suno build has no Exclude Styles field. Trim items from it if you need room for something else.
