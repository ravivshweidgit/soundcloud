**Standing rules**
- One redhead only (`women.png` / `women-face.png`). Never drawn.
- Rain is filmed **on the water** — rings and dimpling in puddles and ruts, never streaks in open air. Every rain shot needs standing water in frame.
- Sun disk withheld until Chorus 3.
- Door: she goes around the gable; camera goes through (dim room + one window OK).
