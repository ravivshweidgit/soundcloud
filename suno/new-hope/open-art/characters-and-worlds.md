# New Hope — characters and worlds (OpenArt asset library)

Concept: `clip-concept.md` · Workflow: `openart-guide.md` · Shot prompts: `openart-paste.md`

These are the descriptions to paste into OpenArt's **Character** and **World** tools, so every
later shot references an asset by tag instead of re-describing the world and drifting.

**One character. Ten worlds.** Eight live-action, two drawn — and two of the live ones (`@river`,
`@glen-bloom`) exist mainly to build the others from.

## How to write these so the glen does not drift

Two rules, learned from the last two films.

**Geography goes in the asset. Light goes in the shot prompt.** The description of a place must
contain only permanent physical facts — stone, slope, direction, what stands where. The moment a
time of day is baked into the asset, every shot using it inherits that time and the film's light
arc dies. Frost, rain, dawn and midnight are all *shot* instructions, listed at the bottom of this
file.

**Every location repeats the shared geography block.** The glen is one place seen seven ways. If
each location is described independently, the model invents a new valley each time. So the block
below goes into all of them, and each location then adds only its own detail.

## THE GLEN — shared geography block

Paste this into every World description, live and drawn, before the location's own paragraph.

```
A narrow glen in the west of Ireland. A single-storey stone cottage stands at the top of the slope with its gable end facing the lane, lime-washed long ago and weathered back to grey, with a slate roof and one small deep-set window. From its door a lane of wet grey stone and packed earth runs downhill, curving once to the left, and ends after about a hundred paces at a field gate set in a drystone wall. Below the lane on the left the ground falls away to a small river running fast over pale pebbles, with a low bank of dark mud on the near side. One wild cherry tree stands immediately beside the cottage, on the right of the lane as you leave the door, growing out of or hard against the stone wall by the gable — this is the landmark tree, the largest and the one nearest the house. It must never appear alone halfway down the lane with empty space around the cottage. Scattered through the rest of the glen stand a handful more wild cherries, five to seven at most: two or three along the river, one or two in the corners of the walled fields, one high on the slope near the ridge. Each stands alone with space around it, self-sown and wind-shaped, never in a row, never a group, never an orchard or a planted avenue. Beyond and around: low rounded hills of rough pasture divided by drystone walls, otherwise treeless, closed by a long ridge line along the horizon. Rushes in the wet hollows, moss and lichen on every stone. No other buildings, no road, no vehicles, no poles, no wires, no fences of wire, no signage.
```

## CHARACTER — `@woman`

The only person in the film. She appears in live action only and is never drawn.

```
A woman in her late twenties to early thirties, of medium height and soft build. Gentle open face, soft features, fair warm skin with a light natural flush on the cheeks — kind and calm, not weathered or harsh. A few sparse light freckles across the bridge of the nose only — never a dense constellation, never covering the forehead or cheeks. No makeup. Clear soft green or grey-green eyes. Natural red hair — copper to auburn — worn loose or softly pulled back with loose strands at the temples, not severe. Her expression is gentle, patient and closed-mouthed — tender rather than sad, and never performing.

She wears a heavy hand-knitted wool jumper in undyed oatmeal cream, thick cable texture, loose and worn soft, the sleeves pushed back at the forearm. A long plain skirt of heavy charcoal wool to mid-calf, hanging straight. Thick dark socks. Scuffed brown leather ankle boots, laced, muddy at the welt. Nothing else: no coat, no shawl, no scarf, no hat, no bag, no jewellery, no watch. No patterns, no prints, no logos. Everything plain, worn and lived in.

Her hands are always empty.

Rendered as filmic live-action photography: naturalistic skin and cloth, soft light falloff, matte finish, muted palette, organic 35mm film grain.

Her mouth stays CLOSED in every shot of the film. No open mouth, no parted lips, no gasp, no gape — an open mouth in a music video reads as singing, and she never sings.

Never: a second person beside her, glamour lighting, heavy makeup, styled runway hair, a shawl over the head, costume-drama styling, claddagh or Celtic jewellery, green novelty Irish clothing, grey streaks or harsh weathered outdoor face, dense freckle constellations covering forehead or cheeks, an open or parted mouth, a gasp or theatrical surprise, anything held in her hands, any illustrated or animated depiction of her.
```

**Her one non-negotiable close-up.** Verse 1 is her eyes and nothing else, so the face has to
survive an extreme close-up: real pores and texture, and **one single small warm catchlight in
each eye** as the only warm thing in the frame. Keep that in the shot prompt, not the asset — it
is light, and it changes across the film.

**The one wardrobe exception — bridge 2's river.** She is booted everywhere in the film except the
midnight walk, where she steps **barefoot** onto the pebbles in the shallow water. That exception is
load-bearing rather than decorative: the footprints she finds in the mud are **boot prints**, so her
being barefoot is what makes "not hers" legible on screen instead of only in the brief. Put the bare
feet in the shot prompt, never in the asset, or she will lose her boots in every other shot.

## WORLD — `@door`

Verse 2 and chorus 2. The border between the two mediums.

```
[shared geography block]

This location is the cottage gable and its door, seen straight on from the lane a few paces back. The door is old, plain, vertical timber boards greyed and grained by weather, with a simple iron latch and no glass, set deep in a thick stone wall under a rough lintel. It is shut. At its foot there is a dark gap between door and threshold stone. The stonework around it is irregular field stone, heavily mossed at the base, with lichen in yellow and grey. A worn flagstone step, hollowed in the middle. Beyond the door's edge the lane begins to fall away downhill. Nothing is stored against the wall, nothing hangs on the door, and the interior beyond is not visible.

Empty location plate, no people and no animals.
```

**Hard rule:** the interior is never seen and never lit. When the camera finally passes through in
bridge 1 it enters **black** — that black frame is where the medium changes.

## WORLD — `@lane`

Verse 2's hundred steps, and the road she takes in chorus 3.

```
[shared geography block]

This location is the lane itself, seen from the cottage end looking down, so that its whole length is legible at once: wet grey stone and packed earth, rutted, puddled in the hollows, with grass growing up the centre. It runs downhill, curves once to the left, and ends plainly at a closed five-bar timber field gate set in a drystone wall — there is nothing beyond the gate but rough pasture. Low drystone walls run along both sides, tumbled in places, thick with moss. The lane is countable: a person could walk its length in about a hundred paces.

Empty location plate, no people and no animals.
```

**Why it matters:** *"Hundred steps away from you. End of the road."* The lane has to read as
finite and the gate has to read as an ending, not an invitation.

## WORLD — `@river`

Both bridges, second movement. Where the footprints are.

```
[shared geography block]

This location is the small river below the lane, seen low and close from the near bank, with the cottage and its landmark cherry visible up the slope on the left and the field gate and drystone walls behind — so the frame proves it is the same place. Fast shallow water running over a bed of pale rounded pebbles and larger dark stones, broken into ripples and small standing waves, the surface dark where it deepens. Two or three more wild cherries stand along the water, each alone, wind-shaped. The near bank is low, bare and soft dark mud, wet and smooth and unmarked, with tufts of rush and grass at its edge — ground that would hold a footprint. The far bank rises steeper, grassy, with exposed stone.

In this version every cherry is BARE — leafless branches, no blossom anywhere.

Empty location plate, no people and no animals, and no footprints in the mud.
```

**This plate is scaffolding, not a shot.** The live river only ever reaches the screen at midnight in
bridge 2, by which point the blossom has happened — so `river.png` exists to hold the geography and
to build `@river-midnight` from. Do not register it as a Director location; it would only tempt the
model into a daylight river that the film never has.

## WORLD — `@river-midnight`

Bridge 2's walk, second half. Where the footprints are. **Build it from the approved `river.png`.**

```
[shared geography block]

This location is that same river below the lane from the identical viewpoint — the same pebble bed, the same dark stones, the same low mud bank on the near side, the same cottage and its landmark cherry up the slope on the left, the same gate and walls behind. Every cherry along the water and beside the cottage is now in FULL WHITE BLOSSOM, same trees, same positions.

It is midnight. A FULL MOON sits in a deep-blue night sky, large enough to read as a disk. Moonlight catches the white blossom so it glows against black banks, and lays a long broken moon-path down the running water. The wet pebbles and the surface of the river hold silver; the banks stay nearly black. Stars are present but small and cold — pinpricks, not gold.

The mud bank is smooth, wet and completely unmarked — no footprints of any kind.

Deep night-blue and silver only. No warm colour anywhere.

Empty location plate, no people and no animals.

Never: pink candy blossom, Japanese sakura, an orchard, trees where the day plate had none, golden or warm moonlight, tungsten colour, HDR glow, light pollution, a second moon, gold-leaf stars, painted or illustrated look, footprints, people, animals, text, watermark.
```

**The footprints stay out of the plate.** They are the payoff of the whole film and they get added in
the shot, so the bank has to be generated clean.

## WORLD — `@cherry-bloom`

Bridge 2's answer to the drawn world.

```
[shared geography block]

This location is the single wild cherry tree beside the cottage, on the right of the lane as you leave the door, seen from the lane a little below it so the tree stands against the hillside and the sky with the cottage gable still in frame beside it. A true wild cherry, gean — a tall slender trunk with smooth banded grey-brown bark marked with horizontal lenticels, branches rising and spreading rather than weeping, in full blossom: small white five-petalled flowers in loose clusters on bare or barely-leafed twigs. Not dense candy-pink, not a cultivated ornamental, not a park tree. It stands hard against the cottage stone and the lane wall — never alone halfway down the lane. Rough pasture around it, and fallen petals scattered on the wet stone of the lane.

Empty location plate, no people and no animals.
```

**Deliberately plainer than the drawn version.** The dream is allowed to be more beautiful than
the real tree. That is the point of having both.

## WORLD — `@glen`

The wide frames: chorus 1's reveal, chorus 3's travel.

```
[shared geography block]

This location is the whole glen seen wide from the opposite hillside, so the geography reads in one frame: the cottage small at the top of the slope with its landmark cherry hard beside it, the lane running down from it and ending at the gate, and the line of the river below with its pale pebble bed. The scattered wild cherries are all present and all clearly visible, each standing alone in its own space — along the river, in the field corners, one high on the slope. Rough pasture in irregular walled fields, rushes in the wet ground, the long ridge closing the horizon. A big open sky over a small inhabited valley.

In this version every cherry is BARE — leafless winter branches, dark and fine against the grass, no blossom and no leaf anywhere. The trees read as shape and position only. The cottage is never shown without its tree beside it.

Empty location plate, no people and no animals.
```

**This plate's real job is to plant the trees.** Their number and their positions are established
here and must not change afterwards. `@glen-bloom` is this exact frame with the same trees in
flower, so anything invented in one has to exist in the other.

## WORLD — `@glen-bloom`

The live-action payoff. **Build it from the approved `glen.png`** so it is unmistakably the same
valley and the same trees.

```
[shared geography block]

This location is that same wide view of the glen from the opposite hillside — the identical cottage, lane, gate, river, ridge line and field walls, from the identical viewpoint — with every one of the scattered wild cherries now in full white blossom. Same trees, same positions, same number, same silhouettes: only the blossom is new. Small white five-petalled wild-cherry flowers in loose clusters on bare or barely-leafed twigs, reading at this distance as clouds of white standing in the green valley. Fallen petals pale on the wet stone of the lane and drifting on the river.

The ground is still soaked from rain, puddles bright in the lane ruts.

Restrained and true, not a fantasy. Muted natural colour, the white of the blossom doing the work rather than saturation. Photoreal live-action, filmic 35mm, matte.

Empty location plate, no people and no animals.

Never: pink candy blossom, Japanese sakura, sakura park or festival, an orchard or planted rows, trees where the bare plate had none, painted or illustrated look, HDR glow, warm fake sunset, the sun disk in frame.
```

**Hard rule:** this frame must stay **less radiant than `@drawn-lane`**. It is the real world
keeping a promise, not the dream. Keep the colour muted, the sun disk out, and let the drawn
sunshower hold the saturation peak.

## WORLD — `@glen-midnight`

Bridge 2's walk opens here. **Build it from the approved `glen-bloom.png`** so it is the same
valley and the same trees in flower — only the light has changed.

```
[shared geography block]

This location is that same wide view of the glen from the opposite hillside — the identical cottage, lane, gate, river, ridge line and field walls, from the identical viewpoint — with every one of the scattered wild cherries still in full white blossom, same trees, same positions, same number. Only the light has changed: it is midnight.

A FULL MOON sits high in a clear deep-blue night sky, large enough to read as a disk, cool and silver. Moonlight rakes the blossom so the white flowers glow against black hills and black cottage stone. The river below catches a long broken moon-path. Stars are present but SMALL and cold — pinpricks, not gold leaf, not decorative. Deep night-blue and silver only; no warm streetlight colour, no gold, no orange.

Empty location plate, no people and no animals.

Never: pink candy blossom, Japanese sakura, sakura park, an orchard, trees where the day plates had none, painted or illustrated look, golden moonlight, warm tungsten colour, HDR glow, city light pollution, a second moon, people, animals, birds, text, watermark.
```

**Hard rule:** this is still the real world, quieter than the drawn dream. Silver and blue, not
gold. The gold stars belong to the painted bridge and to her eyes at the end — not to this sky.

## WORLD — `@drawn-lane`

Bridge 1. **Build this from the approved live plates** so it is unmistakably the same place.

**This is the lane, not the wide valley.** It is the painted counterpart of `lane.png` and
`cherry-bloom.png` — the same cottage corner on the left, the same cherry on the right, the same lane
running down to the same gate. `@drawn-river` is the painted counterpart of the river. Those two are
the whole animated world; there is no painted wide glen.

```
[shared geography block]

This location is that same lane, redrawn — the identical cottage corner and its shut door on the left, the identical drystone walls, the rutted puddled lane running down to the identical field gate, the same hills and ridge line beyond — but rendered as a hand-painted animation background rather than photographed. The landmark cherry on the right is in overwhelming full blossom, far fuller than the real one, and the lane is caught in a sunshower: bright low sunlight and falling rain at the same moment, everything gleaming wet while the sun still strikes it.

Medium: hand-painted gouache and watercolour on heavy paper. Visible paper tooth and texture through the paint, brush edges, wet pigment pooling and blooming at the edges of forms, granulation, passages of dry brush where the paper shows through. Painted light, not photographed light. The rain is drawn as visible slanted lines across the frame; the petals are individual brush strokes on the wet air. Celtic ornament lives quietly in the structure — the interlace logic of knotwork in the branches and roots of the cherry and in the tumbled stone — never as a border or a pasted pattern.

Colour: luminous and saturated, richer than any photograph. White and warm pink blossom, wet green hillside, cool grey-violet rain, gold where the sun cuts through the shower.

Empty location plate, no people, no figures, no silhouettes, no animals.

Never: anime or manga style, Japanese sakura, sakura festival, torii, lanterns, kimono, Ghibli pastiche, cel-shaded gloss, hard black outlines, 3D render, CGI, photorealism, photographic rain blur, vector flatness, comic panels, decorative border.
```

## WORLD — `@drawn-river`

Bridge 1, second movement. Where the stars are gold and there are no footprints.

```
[shared geography block]

This location is that same river below the lane, redrawn in the same hand-painted medium as the drawn lane: the identical viewpoint, the identical pebble bed and dark stones, the same low mud bank in the foreground, the same cottage with its landmark cherry up the slope on the left, the same field gate and drystone walls behind — painted rather than photographed. Every cherry is in overwhelming full blossom, fuller than the real ones. The water is drawn in confident strokes with white ripple marks, the pebbles as deliberate individual touches of colour. Scattered across the surface of the water, points of light are laid in real gold leaf, flat and slightly uneven, catching as metal rather than painted yellow — stars lying on the water. Blossom petals drift on the surface as small brush strokes.

Medium: hand-painted gouache and watercolour on heavy paper with visible paper tooth, brush edges, pooling pigment and dry-brush passages, plus gold leaf. Painted light. Celtic interlace logic in the pattern of the ripples and in the roots along the bank.

The mud bank is smooth, wet and completely unmarked — there are no footprints of any kind.

Empty location plate, no people, no figures, no silhouettes, no animals.

Never: anime or manga style, Japanese sakura, Ghibli pastiche, cel-shaded gloss, 3D render, CGI, photorealism, vector flatness, text, calligraphy, letterforms.
```

## LIGHT STATES — put these in the shot prompt, never in the asset

| Tag | Light | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| **L1 pre-dawn** | Cold blue-grey before sunrise, no direct sun, flat and shadowless, low contrast, pale cool sky | Verse 1, verse 2 |
| **L2 first gold** | Sun still below the ridge but the sky warming; the first gold touching only the top edges of stone and the cottage roof; shadows still cold and winning | Chorus 1, chorus 2 |
| **L3 full morning** | The sun clear of the ridge, disk allowed in frame, long raking warm light down the lane, wet stone flaring, air bright — the warmest photographed frame in the film, but still true rather than triumphant | Chorus 3 |
| **L4 winter rain** | Cold grey overcast, wet stone and mud, bare leafless scrub. **Rain is a video instruction, and it is filmed on the water** — puddles and flooded ruts held large in frame, rings spreading and overlapping on the surface, drips off stone. Never falling streaks in open air. Do not bake rain into a still plate | Bridge 1 wait, bridge 2 wait |
| **L5 live sunshower** | **Low sun breaking through broken cloud while it is still raining**, raking the blossom — Bridge 2 sits after Chorus 3, so the real world has already warmed. Keep a puddle in the lower frame so the rain reads as lit rings on water. Sun disk off frame. The real answer to L7, and it must stay cooler and plainer than it | Bridge 2, the cherry |
| **L6 midnight** | Deep night blue, **full moon allowed as a disk**, moonlight only, blossom and water catching silver, stars present but small and cold, no gold | Bridge 2 walk — glen wide, then the river |
| **L7 drawn sunshower** | Painted sun and painted rain at once, gold leaf, the most saturated passage in the film — drawn only | Bridge 1, both drawn worlds |

**The rule that keeps the arc alive:** L3, the best real light in the film, must still sit *below*
L7 in beauty and saturation. Real morning is true; only the dream is radiant.

is why the arrangement note holds — the fiddle-led bridges are where the place takes over. And
`@door` never appears after bridge 1, because once we have passed through it, it has done its job.
