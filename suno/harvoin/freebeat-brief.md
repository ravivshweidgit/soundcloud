## Setup

- Mode: **Storytelling** (not Stage Performance — there is no narrative singer here, the song is a landscape and a wait).
- Lyrics: **paste manually** from `lyrics-suno.md`. Do not rely on auto-transcription; sung Finnish gets mangled and the invented-syllable version will come back.
- Aspect: 16:9 master for YouTube. Freebeat offers multiple ratios per render, so pick
  9:16 at the same time rather than re-rendering for a vertical cut later.
- Pacing: slow. Long holds, few cuts. Let the chorus repeat build hypnosis instead of adding new events.

## Logline (paste into the concept field)

A lone figure returns to a wide northern river plain and waits through a night that never
fully darkens. Two people rarely meet in these poor borderlands — the film is the waiting,
not the meeting.

## Visual style prompt

Nordic folk realism, wide flat Ostrobothnian river plain, low horizon, tall grass and
reeds, wooden fences, grey timber barns, slow river water. Midnight sun light: pale gold
and cold blue at once, no full darkness, long soft shadows. Muted desaturated palette,
natural light only, 35mm film grain, static or very slow camera. Melancholic, patient,
reverent. No text, no logos, no modern city, no cars.

## Character

One figure, seen mostly from behind or in silhouette — dark wool coat, plain clothes,
no era-specific detail. Never a close-up hero shot until the final section. The land is
the lead; the figure is scale.

Freebeat's character consistency across scenes is unreliable, so this is deliberate:
back-views, silhouettes and distant figures hide face drift between clips. Do not write
prompts that depend on recognising the same face twice.

## Notes on meaning (so the AI does not invent the wrong story)

- "Harvoin yhteen yhtymme" is about **rarely meeting**, not reunion. Do **not** generate an
  embrace, a kiss, or a happy meeting.
- "raukoilla rajoilla / poloisilla Pohjan mailla" is **hardship and poverty**, not tourism.
  Avoid pretty postcard scenery.
- "keskiyön aurinkomaa" = **midnight sun**, so never full night. Keep the sky lit.
- Geographic tension is intentional: Etelä-Pohjanmaa is flat farmland, midnight sun is
  further north. Blend them — flat plain under a sun that will not set.

## Avoid

people dancing, crowds, concert stage, lip sync, neon, cityscape, cars, modern fashion,
subtitles or lyric text burned in, fantasy creatures, Viking or metal-band imagery,
tropical or desert light, full darkness.
